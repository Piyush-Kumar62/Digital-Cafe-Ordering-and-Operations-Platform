Cookie Policy

This policy explains how Digital Café uses cookies and similar technologies to keep your account secure, remember your preferences, and improve the platform experience.

🍪

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. They help us recognize your browser, keep you signed in, and remember choices you make.

🔐

Essential Cookies

Required for authentication, session security, CSRF protection, and role-based access. These cookies are always on.

📈

Analytics Cookies

Help us understand traffic and feature usage so we can improve booking, ordering, and staff workflows. Stored only with consent.

🎯

Marketing Cookies

Used to measure campaigns and personalize updates. These cookies are optional and disabled by default.

🧭

How We Use Cookies

  • Maintain secure sign-in sessions and protect against fraud.
  • Remember preferences like theme, language, and device settings.
  • Measure performance and improve user experience (with consent).
🛠️

Managing Preferences

Update your preferences anytime using “Open Cookie Settings” above. Clearing browser data may reset your choices.

🗄️

Retention & Storage

Cookies expire automatically after a defined period or when you clear browser data. We avoid storing unnecessary personal data in cookies.

Contact

For privacy questions, email privacy@digitalcafe.com.

🔒

Security & Compliance

We protect cookie data with access controls and follow applicable privacy regulations for consent and storage.
