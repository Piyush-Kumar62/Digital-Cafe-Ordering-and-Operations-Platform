Data Deletion Request

You can request deletion of your Digital Café account and associated personal data. This page explains the steps and what is retained for compliance.

📝

How to Request Deletion

  1. Email privacy@digitalcafe.com with the subject “Data Deletion Request”.
  2. Include your account email and role (Customer / Owner / Chef / Waiter).
  3. We may verify your identity before processing the request.
🧹

What We Delete

  • Profile information (name, phone, address, avatar)
  • Non‑essential preferences and settings
  • Marketing consent records
🗄️

What We May Retain

To comply with legal and financial obligations, some transaction or audit records may be retained in a restricted, non‑public form.

⏳

Processing Time

Requests are typically completed within 7–14 business days.
