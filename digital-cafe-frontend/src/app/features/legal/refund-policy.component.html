Refund & Cancellation Policy

This policy outlines how refunds work for bookings, orders, and payments made through Digital Café.

📅

Bookings

  • Bookings can be cancelled based on the café’s cancellation window.
  • Late cancellations may incur a fee or be non-refundable.
  • Refunds are processed to the original payment method.
🧾

Orders

  • Orders already prepared or served are not refundable.
  • If an order is cancelled before preparation, a full refund applies.
  • Incorrect or damaged orders can be reported for review.
⏱️

Processing Time

Approved refunds are typically processed within 5–7 business days, depending on your payment provider.

✉️

Contact

For refund questions, contact support@digitalcafe.com.
